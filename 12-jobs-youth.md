## Lab 

In this lab, we will use (some of) the data from the paper 
[A Firm of One's Own: Experimental Evidence on Credit Constraints and Occupational Choice](https://direct.mit.edu/rest/article-abstract/doi/10.1162/rest_a_01529/125020/A-Firm-of-One-s-Own-Experimental-Evidence-on?redirectedFrom=fulltext), which you read before class. The data that we will use for this lab is available [here](ECON204-gem-data.dta).  

You can complete the lab in either Stata or R, though I recommend Stata for first and second year students planning to major in economics. Guidance on how to complete the lab is available for [Stata](ECON204-youth-lab.pdf) or [R](ECON204-youth-lab-R.pdf). The Stata template is available [here](ECON-204-youth-lab-template.do), and the R template is available [here](ECON-204-youth-lab-template.R).
