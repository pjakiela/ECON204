## Lab 

In this lab, we will test the geography hypothesis using [data on GDP per capita and country-level geographic characteristics](ECON204-geography-lab-data.dta).  

You can complete the lab in either Stata or R, though I recommend Stata for first and second year students planning to major in economics. If you are new to Stata, 
be sure to watch [Stata tutorials](https://pjakiela.github.io/stata/) [0](https://pjakiela.github.io/stata/mod0.html), [1](https://pjakiela.github.io/stata/openingData.html), 
[4](https://pjakiela.github.io/stata/variablesMod.html), and [9](https://pjakiela.github.io/stata/analyzingdatastructures.html) before starting the lab.  If you are new to R, 
I provide some guidance on getting started in R [here](https://pjakiela.github.io/ECON370/getting-started-R.html). The lab templates will also coach you through all of the steps 
required to complete the lab.  

The Stata template is available [here](ECON204-geography-lab-template.do).  

The R template is available [here](ECON204-geography-lab-template.R).
